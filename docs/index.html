Calculated, not invented

Most tarot apps pick a sentence at random. Arcana builds every reading from what was actually drawn — the card and its orientation, the position it landed in, how many Major Arcana appeared, how many cards are reversed, and the balance of the four elements across the spread. Yes-or-No answers follow each card's traditional polarity, not a coin flip. Interpretations follow the Rider–Waite tradition.
