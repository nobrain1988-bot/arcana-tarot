Arcana — card artwork credits
============================================================

Rider-Waite-Smith Tarot (1909)
Artwork by Pamela Colman Smith (1878-1951), under the direction of A. E. Waite.

PUBLIC DOMAIN.
  - United States: published 1909, copyright expired.
  - EU / UK / South Korea: author died 1951; life + 70 years expired 1 January 2022.

Source: Wikimedia Commons. Every file below carries a public-domain licence tag
on its own description page, linked here as evidence.

Note: this is the ORIGINAL 1909 artwork. Modern recoloured or restored editions
(e.g. Centennial, Radiant) carry fresh copyright on the restoration and are NOT used.
